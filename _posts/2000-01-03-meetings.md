## Board Meetings

- SUVHOA Board Meetings are scheduled on every second Wednesday of each month at 6:30pm.
  * *Meeting Location: SUVHOA Clubhouse (7290 Davenport Rd).*
- Homeowners are welcome to attend.
  

**Please visit the [TeamHOA Portal](https://teamhoa.com) for agendas, minutes, and financials.**
