# **Board Meetings**


#### SUVHOA Board Meetings are scheduled for every second Wednesday of each month at 6:30 p.m. Meetings are held at the SUVHOA Clubhouse at 7290 Davenport Rd., Goleta, CA 93117

## **Meeting Information** 
#### Board members meet on a monthly basis to discuss neighborhood issues, amenities maintenance and repairs, and more. Homeowners are welcome to attend our meetings.
